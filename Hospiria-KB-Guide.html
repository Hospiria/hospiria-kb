Hospiria Knowledge Base · Feature & Admin Guide

What the Knowledge Base can do

A plain-English tour of everything that's been built — for managers and admins.

The Hospiria Knowledge Base is where the team finds how we do things. On top of standard SOP articles, it now has: a tagging system for clients and software, fast search and browsing, an AI chat assistant that answers from our SOPs, a tool that auto-assigns SOPs to clients, and an AI Training area where we teach the assistant our ways and turn real team chats into knowledge. This guide walks through each one.

Who can see what. Everyone who is signed in can browse SOPs, use search, and use the chat assistant. The Admin sections (Companies, Platforms, Import, Auto-tag, AI Training, Users) are admin-only. What SOPs a person sees also depends on their role — front-line agents only see published ("live") SOPs, while managers/admins can see drafts too.

1.Companies & Platforms

Admin only Admin → Companies / Platforms

Two kinds of label you can attach to any SOP so the right knowledge reaches the right place:

  • Companies — the client or brand an SOP is for (e.g. Get Living, Under The Doormat). An SOP can belong to several.
  • Platforms — the software or channel an SOP relates to (e.g. PriceLabs, Rentals United, Airbnb).

Manage them under Admin → Companies and Admin → Platforms: add, rename, deactivate, or delete tags. There's a CSV import for adding a long list of names in one go (one name per line), which automatically skips duplicates.

Tip: prefer Deactivate over Delete. Deactivating hides a tag from the dropdowns but keeps it on past SOPs; deleting removes it from every SOP permanently.

2.Browsing & search

All users All SOPs page + left sidebar

Find SOPs two ways: type to search, or filter/browse.

  • Live search on the All SOPs page updates results as you type. It matches both partial words in titles (typing “hand” finds “Handling…”) and words anywhere in the article body, so you catch things whether you remember the title or just a phrase inside.
  • Filter bar — narrow by Team, Company, Platform, and (for managers/admins) draft vs live Status. Filters combine.
  • Sidebar browsing — the Library section lists Companies and Platforms; click one to see its SOPs. Each list has its own search box for when there are many.

3.The AI chat assistant

All users Floating bubble, bottom-right of every page

A chat bubble that answers questions using our SOPs — like asking a knowledgeable colleague. Key things it does:

  • Answers from our SOPs and shows clickable source links so you can verify where an answer came from.
  • Client-aware. Because processes differ per client, it will ask which company you mean before giving a process-specific answer, and can draft a ready-to-send guest message in that client's tone.
  • Stays grounded — it's instructed never to invent fees or times, and to say so (and suggest escalating) when no SOP covers your question.
  • Replies are nicely formatted (bold, bullets, numbered steps). Your chat history is saved and private to you; you can start a new chat any time.
Good to know: the assistant is only as good as the SOPs it can find. If a topic isn't documented for a client, it will say so rather than guess.

4.Auto-assigning SOPs to clients

Admin only Admin → Auto-tag SOPs

A time-saver for tagging a big back-catalogue of SOPs to the right Company. Instead of opening each SOP by hand, it scans them for you and lets you approve in bulk.

1

Scan. It reads every SOP that has no company yet and looks for client names in the title and text, sorting them into Confident (pre-ticked), Needs review (a likely guess), and No match.

2

Optional AI pass. For the leftovers with no obvious name match, you can run an AI step that reads them and suggests a client. Its suggestions land in “Needs review” for you to check.

3

Review & apply. You tick/untick the suggestions, then click Apply checked. Nothing is tagged until you apply, and re-running won't create duplicates.

5.AI Training — teaching the assistant

Admin only Admin → AI Training → “Behaviour” tab

Where you teach the chat assistant how to behave, in plain English. Three sections:

SectionWhat goes here
PrinciplesOur ways — tone, rules of thumb, what to ask first (e.g. “always include the cleaning fee when quoting”).
People & rolesWho's who, so the bot can point people to the right person (e.g. “Sonali approves rate/discount changes”).
Guardrails & fallbacksHard rules and what to do when there's no SOP (e.g. “if unsure about a discount, ask a manager before quoting”).

Add or edit items and save. These rules are attached to the assistant's instructions on every message, so changes take effect on the next chat — no waiting, no deploy. The bot always keeps its core grounding (never invent fees), so these rules guide it rather than override safety.

Note: saving a section replaces that section's whole list, so edit the full list, then save.

6.Teach from conversations

Admin only Admin → AI Training → “Teach from conversations” tab

Turn a real team chat (e.g. a WhatsApp conversation) into knowledge: it finds reusable processes worth writing up as SOPs, and “our ways” advice worth adding to the assistant's Behaviour. You review and choose what to keep.

Privacy first. Personal and sensitive details are stripped on our own server, before any text reaches the AI. Automatically removed: phone numbers, emails, links, postcodes, booking references, and door / lockbox / key / gate / WiFi codes. After analysing you'll see a count like 137 items redacted so you know it ran. The AI is also told never to repeat guest names or addresses in anything it drafts.

How to use it

1

Add the chat. Paste it, or click Upload .txt and choose a WhatsApp export. You can drop a whole export.

In WhatsApp: open the chat → menu → More → Export chat → Without media → save the .txt.

2

Click “Analyse conversation”. Long chats are processed in parts (you'll see “Analysing part 3 of 20…”), and slow parts retry on their own so the whole chat is covered.

3

Review what it found (see the table below). Edit any wording before saving.

4

Keep only what you want — tick items and click Create … as drafts (SOPs) or Add … to Behaviour (rules). You don't have to save anything.

5

Start over any time with the Start over / Discard & start over buttons, or just upload a new file (which clears the last run).
